## What support can help with

- Account access and beta onboarding steps
- Paper-trading UI/API defects
- Clarifying that data is delayed/EOD and that fills are simulated
- Incident status for confirmed outages

## What support cannot do

- Place or broker live trades
- Provide personalized investment advice
- Bypass licensed data entitlements
- Promise returns or “fix” historical paper results
